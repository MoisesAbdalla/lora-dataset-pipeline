# Shot list — 25 images for a character LoRA

Example subject: a fictional 3D mascot. Generate 2–3 variations of each prompt and keep only the
best one. The mix below (8 close-ups, 9 waist-up, 8 full body) is what teaches identity *and*
lets the LoRA generalize to new framings.

**Prefix (prepended to every prompt):**
> A single image of one character, the same character from the reference images: a small orange fox mascot in a stylized 3D animation style. Keep its exact face, eye shape, eye color, fur colors and markings identical. Body: round chubby body, short legs, big fluffy tail with a white tip. The references are face close-ups only: do not copy their pose or framing; follow the outfit and pose described below.

**Negative prompt (always the same):**
> collage, grid, multiple characters, duplicate, different character, changed face, photorealistic, blurry, deformed, extra limbs, watermark, text

## Close-up (8) — what teaches identity the most

2. [close-up] Tight head-and-shoulders close-up, wearing a light blue scarf, head turned 45 degrees to the left, slight smile, soft daylight, blurred indoor background.
3. [close-up] Tight head-and-shoulders close-up, wearing a beige sweater, head turned 45 degrees to the right, serious expression, golden hour light, outdoors.
4. [close-up] Side profile view (90 degrees), wearing a black turtleneck, studio lighting, dark background.
5. [close-up] Tight head-and-shoulders close-up, laughing with mouth open, eyes squinted, bright natural light.
6. [close-up] Low angle, looking up, wearing a denim jacket, overcast sky.
7. [close-up] High angle, looking down at a phone, wearing a gray hoodie, cozy lamp light.
8. [close-up] Wearing a knitted beanie, surprised expression, snowy background.
